# Project review sheets

Use a review sheet for a small, deliberate set of definitions, results, notes and questions you want to revisit in one project.

## Choose items

1. Open a project and choose **Review sheet**, or use Home's **Review sheet** shortcut for the selected project.
2. Choose **Choose items**. Search by note, paper or author.
3. Check the items you want and choose **Save selection**.

You can also use **More → Add to review sheet** on an annotation, result or research note. Choose a project if asked. Items need to belong to that project; result drafts need acceptance before entering its main key items. General pins remain independent.

## Read the sheet

**Key items** shows your selected mathematics and notes. Statements render math; annotations retain source images. Personal thoughts and proof/adaptation writing are available in collapsed details. Use **Open note** for the original and **Original passage** for its PDF source.

**Open questions** shows selected unresolved questions that do not already belong beside a mathematical key item. Related questions can appear with their key item instead. The native Outline sidebar lists the sheet's headings.

The sheet reads current notes rather than making copies. Your edits appear on the sheet. Renames and restored notes keep their selections.

## Resolve a selected question

Use **Resolve question** on the sheet or in the note. The open question disappears from that part of the sheet, while its text remains in the source note. A mathematical key item's card can remain. Editing the question text reopens it. To find resolved questions globally, use [[Help/Questions|Questions]] and its search instructions.

Typing a question does not automatically add the note to a curated sheet. Select it first, or use Home's **Open questions** to see questions beyond sheet selections.

## Remove a selection without losing a note

Use the card's **More → Remove from review sheet**, or uncheck it in **Choose items → Save selection**. The note and PDF mark remain. Individual removal shows **Removed from sheet. Note kept.**, with **Open note** and **Undo**.

If you cannot find an item afterward, use **Find notes** on the sheet or in search. Clearing filters broadens the scope. Unaccepted selections and items moved outside the project are kept under **Needs attention**; review them there rather than assuming their notes were deleted.

For relationships between notes use [[Help/Result connections|Connections]]. For a personal reminder use [[Help/Search and review#Pin or classify an item|Pin for review]].
