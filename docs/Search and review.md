# Search and recall

Start on [[Home|Library]] for a paper, or open [[Search and review|Search and review]] for notes, passages and review views.

## Find a paper

Home's **Find papers** searches title, author and filename. Check the **Project**, **Document type**, and **Source collection** filters if a paper seems missing.

For a narrower search, open **Search and review → Find a paper**. Choose **Match → Author** or **Paper title**. Author searches support names and initials, such as Martin Kassabov, Kassabov, Martin, or M. Kassabov.

## Find your writing or a saved result

1. Choose **Find notes**.
2. Enter words from the note's title, filename, your writing, question or captured passage. Use **Match → Any words** for note titles; **Paper title** restricts matching to the source paper's title.
3. Open the matching item, or use **Original passage** to return to its source.

Several search words must all match somewhere in the indexed item. Put a phrase in quotation marks, such as "spectral gap". Note-title searches recognize aliases such as C_i, Ci and Cᵢ. Formula text is searched as written; this is keyword search, without semantic AI matching. Text elsewhere in a PDF is found through the PDF reader.

## Choose a review view

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| All research | Items within your current search and filters. |
| Pinned for review | Items you explicitly pinned. |
| Definitions and results | Mathematical items; saved-result drafts need acceptance to enter this view. |
| Questions | Notes with questions; defaults to open questions. |
| Needs review | Items whose metadata or result review needs attention. |

Use **Filters** for project, item type, mathematical type, question state and sorting. Set **Sort by → Recently edited** to return to recent writing. To include dismissed result drafts, enable that option in Filters.

**Find notes** removes restrictive paper/review filters and keeps your keywords. **Clear filters** broadens the scope to everything and also keeps the keywords; clear the search field separately if needed. See [[Help/Troubleshooting|Troubleshooting]] when an expected item is absent.

## Pin or classify an item

On an annotation/result, or a search card, choose **More → Pin for review**. Use **Unpin from review** to remove that reminder. Pinning keeps the original note and is separate from a project's review sheet.

**More → Review settings** lets you choose the mathematical type, pin state and question state. For example, classify a useful annotated definition as **Definition** so it is easier to recall. Suggested results must be accepted before pinning. See [[Help/Result reuse|result review states]].

## Three ways to revisit material

Use a pin for a personal reminder, a [[Help/Project review|review sheet]] for a project's chosen essentials, and [[Help/Result connections|connections]] to trace relationships. None of these duplicates or deletes the underlying note.
