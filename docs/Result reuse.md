# Saving and adapting results

Save a result when you need an editable definition, theorem, lemma or proof idea, especially when you will use it in another argument.

## Save a source-linked result

1. Open its annotation and choose **More → Save a result**. You can also use **Save a result** on Home or a project page.
2. Give it a short descriptive **Title**. Choose **Use directly** or **Adapt it**.
3. Check **Source and details**: kind, project, paper and physical PDF page. A source annotation supplies its captured passage and image.
4. Choose **Save and write**.

The note opens ready for Markdown/LaTeX. **Statement** is for the published statement and its assumptions. **My work** holds your understanding and use. **Source** returns to the original passage and paper. The statement is not automatically filled from unreliable PDF text extraction; write it yourself or use [[Help/AI transcription|passage transcription]].

**Open source** follows a source annotation after restoration or renaming when its capture is uniquely identifiable. If that annotation is unavailable, it opens the saved page instead; a different PDF version is refused.

Older saved notes may use **Published statement** and **Notes**. Their populated writing remains available; you do not need to recreate them.

## Adapt assumptions or proof

For a new result, choose **Adapt it** while saving. For an existing one, use **More → Adapt this result**, or **Continue adaptation** if already started.

Keep the source statement intact. Write your **Proposed statement**, **Changed assumptions**, and **Proof changes** under **My work**. Older notes gain corresponding adaptation sections while retaining existing writing. Use **Write my work** to return to the editor. An adaptation is labeled a draft; a saved reference does not establish that your modified argument is valid.

## Understand the review states

| State | Meaning and action |
| --- | --- |
| Suggested / Saved | **Accept for reference** keeps a suggestion for recall. **Return to suggestions** reverses that choice without deleting the note. |
| Transcription unchecked / checked | Compare the statement with the source, then choose **I checked the transcription**. Use **Mark transcription unchecked** if further correction is needed. |
| Mathematics status | A separate recorded judgment, shown under **Details**. Saving or checking transcription does not automatically verify a proof. |
| Origin | The compact line distinguishes your note, manual transcription, assistant material and AI transcription. **Details** retains further provenance. |

Manual **Save and write** creates a saved result with transcription initially unchecked. Passage transcription creates a suggestion for review. Check the compact line rather than assuming either action has proved or checked the mathematics.

## Ask, connect and recall

**More → Write question** adds an optional **Questions to check** section with open/resolved controls. See [[Help/Questions|Questions]].

Use **More → Connect results** for Uses/Used by links or related annotation questions. Use **More → Add to review sheet** for a project's curated selection, or **Pin for review** for a reminder. **Copy citation** and **Add to citation list** refer to the source paper; see [[Help/Citations|Citations]].
